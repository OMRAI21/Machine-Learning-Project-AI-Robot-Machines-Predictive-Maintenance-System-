How it works

  • Failure risk: a random forest estimates the chance a robot fails within the next 14 days.
  • Remaining life: a second forest estimates days until failure, capped at 120.
  • Likely fault: a third forest picks the most probable cause: bearing wear, gearbox wear, motor overheating or lubrication loss.
  • Why flagged: each sensor is compared with the average and spread of healthy robots.
  • Status: Critical at 50% risk or 14 days left. Watch at 15% risk or 45 days left.
  • Retraining: replace simulate_fleet() in train_model.py with a loader for your real logs (one row per robot per day, plus days to failure) and run it. It writes a new model.json.
